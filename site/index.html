How to play

  • Jump: tap the screen, click, or press Space/↑. Tap again in the air to double-jump.
  • Hold for a higher jump. Tall standpipes need it.
  • Slide: swipe down, tap SLIDE, or press ↓ to get under low main lines.
  • Time the geysers: run past when they're off, or double-jump over the spray.
  • Grab drops (+10) in a row to build a combo up to x5. Golden valves are worth +150.
  • Power-ups: Pressure Regulator blocks one hit, Smart Controller pulls drops toward you, Flow Meter doubles points.
  • It gets faster the farther you go. Your best score each month counts.
