Contest rules

Drip Run Monthly Challenge (the endless runner Drip Run and its leaderboard) is a skill-based game run by inCon-trol Water Systems ("inCon-trol"). No purchase is necessary to play or win. New worlds are added to the game during the contest; every month's ranking uses the worlds that are live that month.

  1. Eligibility. Open to employees of inCon-trol customers and partners who are 18 or older. inCon-trol employees may play but are not eligible for prizes.
  2. Contest period. Each calendar month (US Central Time) is a separate contest. The leaderboard keeps every month's history and an all-time ranking.
  3. How to win. Your highest verified score in a month is your entry. Ties go to whoever reached the score first.
  4. Verification. Scores are checked automatically for plausibility, and top scores are reviewed by inCon-trol before winners are announced. We may disqualify scores that look automated, modified or otherwise unfair, and we may ask the winner to confirm their email address.
  5. Prizes. [Prize for 1st / 2nd / 3rd place, approximate retail value]. Winners are announced in the following month's newsletter. [One prize per company per month.]
  6. One account per person. Use your own business email. Duplicate or shared accounts may be removed.
  7. Display names. 3 to 24 characters, unique, and suitable for a public leaderboard. inCon-trol may change or remove names that are offensive, misleading or impersonate someone.
  8. Changes. inCon-trol may change or end the game at any time.

Privacy notice

  • What we collect: your display name, business email, company, your game scores and run times, and a one-way hash of your IP address for spam protection.
  • What's public: only your display name, company and scores. Your email is never shown on the leaderboard.
  • How we use it: to run the leaderboard, verify and contact winners and, if you opted in, to send you our newsletter. We don't sell your information.
  • Removal: email [marketing@incontrolwatersystems.com] to have your data deleted.
