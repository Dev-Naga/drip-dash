How to play

  • Move Spritz: arrow keys or WASD; or hold the mouse button and point; on a phone, drag anywhere to steer.
  • Dash: Space, Shift, right-click or the DASH button. You can't be hit mid-dash. Recharges in 2 s.
  • Read the warnings: shadows = falling drops, bubbling rings = geysers, red dashed lines = jets, yellow dotted arm = the Big Fountain, swirling rings = drains pulling.
  • New attractions switch on as you survive: fountain carts, rotor sprinklers and splash waves (find the gap).
  • Umbrellas are safe zones from falling water: drops and sprinkler spray can't reach you under one.
  • Golden drops +25, in a row up to x5. Power-ups: Bubble Shield, Smart Controller (slow-mo), Drop Magnet, Refill.
  • You have 3 drops of health. 10 points per second survived. Your best run each month counts.
