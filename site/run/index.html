Drip Dash

Water Run

Race the sunset along the Water Trail.

The fountains on the Water Trail are running dry. Drip, the last drop out of Melissa Lake Park's tipping bucket, has until nightfall to reach Commons at Agora and switch on the evening show, waking every water feature on the way.

How to play

  • Change lanes: ← → / A D, swipe left/right, or click the left/right side of the screen.
  • Jump: ↑ / W / Space, swipe up, or tap/click the middle. Clears low pipes, weir walls and spraying drain grates.
  • Slide: ↓ / S or swipe down. Gets you under water arches and beams.
  • Fountain basins and water walls fill the lane: switch lanes. Geysers flash red before they erupt.
  • Your mission: deliver water. Drops fill Drip's water tank (it slowly evaporates). Each stop has 3 glowing supply inlets: run through one with at least 35% water to switch on the project's fountains ahead (+150). Supply all 3 to complete the stop (+300). Skipped sections stay dry.
  • At Aggie Park and downtown, people walk along the path: change lanes to go around them.
  • Clip the side of something and Drip bounces back once. A second bump within 4 s ends the run.
  • Drops +10, golden valves +100. Power-ups: Pressure Regulator (shield), Smart Controller (drop magnet), Flow Meter (x2 points), Jet Boost (super speed, smash through anything).
  • The journey: 8 stops, each a real inCon-trol project, from sunrise at Melissa Lake Park to the evening show at Commons at Agora (2,400 m). Fountains come back to life as you pass them. Make it to Agora, then keep going for a high score!
  • Project signs: tap or click the glowing sign (or the ⓘ button, or press I) to pause and read about the project. Play resumes with a 3-2-1 count when you close it.
  • Your outfit from Drip Dash comes with you!
